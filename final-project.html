Final Project · Commodity trends and covered calls

Thesis. I believe three-month price trends across metals, energy and grains persist into the following month often enough that following their direction can outperform an equal-weight long position after trading costs, with covered calls improving bullish trades when premiums exceed the upside surrendered.

Tracking the signal

Each line shows the percentage change from 63 common trading sessions ago. Above zero favors a long position and below zero favors a short. The chart updates daily. The strategy uses the previous session’s reading at the close of the first common trading day each month, assigns one sixth of the portfolio’s notional exposure to each market, and holds that direction until the next review. An exact zero leaves that allocation in cash.

Options on futures · covered calls

On a bullish monthly signal, pair one long futures contract with one short call on that same delivery month and contract size. Choose the listed strike nearest 5% above entry and an expiry nearest the next monthly review. On short or flat signals, skip the call. Close both legs together by expiry or before delivery restrictions. This adapts the covered-call assignment to futures.

A quiet rally lets the strategy collect premium and keep most of the futures gain. A sharp rally gives up gains above the strike. A falling market still loses on the long future, cushioned only by the premium received.

How much premium would the call need?

For each completed bullish month, the tracker measures how far the futures return exceeded 5%, floors that loss at zero, then adds an assumed 0.10% of entry notional for option trading costs. The table averages that amount across trades. A hurdle of 1% means the short call would have needed an average entry premium of $1 per $100 of underlying exposure to match futures alone.

Time horizon

My initial research horizon is 12–24 months. I expect a repricing after a crop or monetary shock to last weeks to months. Each signal commits capital for about 21 trading days. If the sign persists, the same directional position carries into the next month. I will review the evidence after 12 new monthly decisions.

How large is the edge?

The comparison uses equal daily notional weights and close-to-close returns, with each new direction earning returns from the session after execution. I charge 5 basis points per one-way change in notional for spread, fees and slippage. Reversing one market trades one third of portfolio notional. The estimate is the annualized mean daily return difference versus a daily rebalanced equal-weight long basket, before financing and collateral interest.

Yahoo Finance’s nearby futures series can jump when the underlying contract changes. The estimate omits explicit contract rolls and their costs. A small measured edge can disappear when tested on individual expiries.

When I would drop the thesis

  • After 12 new monthly decisions, the strategy’s cumulative net return relative to the long basket is zero or negative.
  • Across those 12 decisions, signals in at least four of six markets individually predict the sign of the following holding-period return at most half the time.
  • Drop the call overlay if, after 12 completed bullish option trades with observed quotes, net premium collected fails to cover aggregate call losses.

Why a trend might persist

The basket spans COMEX gold and copper, NYMEX WTI crude, and CBOT corn, soybeans and Chicago wheat. Copper responds to industrial demand, crude to changes in supply and inventories, and grains to planting and harvest surprises. A sustained rise in real interest rates raises the opportunity cost of holding gold, while a weaker dollar can support its dollar price. Corn supply responds slowly to drought because a lost harvest takes a growing season to replace. Those adjustments can extend a move across several monthly reviews. A sudden Fed reversal or a favorable crop forecast can erase it before the next review. During a tech selloff, demand for gold as a haven can compete with forced sales to raise cash, so the signal follows the observed price direction.
